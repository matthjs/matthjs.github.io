### Student Project Supervision

**University of Groningen**

In the **AI Engineering Practical (2025–2026)**, I supported four teams working on industry projects. This involved weekly advisor meetings with the teams throughout their internships, staying in contact with company stakeholders, and giving feedback on the teams' progress. The teams worked with [UMCG](https://www.umcg.nl/), [Waterlaboratorium Noord](https://wln.nl/), [Gasunie](https://www.gasunie.nl/), and [AI-InfraSolutions](https://ai-infrasolutions.com/) on projects involving PET/CT-based toxicity prediction, drinking-water quality alerts, electricity-price forecasting, and streetlight detection from mobile-mapping data.

In the **Reinforcement Learning Practical (2024–2025)**, I supervised multiple student research projects in (deep) reinforcement learning.
