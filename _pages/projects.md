**Pokémon TCG AI Battle Challenge** · [Competition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/pokemon-tcg-ai-battle/overview) · [Code](https://github.com/squidistaken/pokemon-tcg-ai)  
We trained an agent to play the Pokémon Trading Card Game using behavioral cloning, PPO, and self-play. The solution placed 168th of 6,807 teams.

**NS-Gym Competition** · [Competition](https://nsgym.io/aamas2026_competition.html) · [Code](https://github.com/Jazhyc/nsgym-solution)  
A PPO-based solution for adapting to changing environments, ranked first in the competition's adaptability, performance, resilience, and efficiency evaluations.

**Aletheia’s Quest** · [Competition results](https://aletheias-quest.github.io/winners.html) · [Code](https://github.com/Jazhyc/aletheia-quest-sain-groningen)  
Representing [SAIN Groningen](https://safeainetherlands.org/chapters/groningen), we developed a black-box deception monitor by distilling Kimi K3's judgments into a Qwen3.5-9B LoRA, as well as a white-box extension combining the monitor with an activation probe. Our black-box submission placed second in its track and won the Scalability Award.

**Semantic Preservation in Discrete Diffusion Models** · [Code](https://github.com/matthjs/udl-diffusion-semantics)  
We trained discrete diffusion models in VQ-VAE latent space and compared how uniform and absorbing-state corruption preserve image-class information across diffusion timesteps on MNIST and CIFAR-10.

**Gaussian Process Proximal Policy Optimization (GPPO)** · [Code](https://github.com/matthjs/gppo)  
A model-free actor–critic method using deep Gaussian processes to approximate the policy and value function. This was part of a research project I did for the Deep Reinforcement Learning course at the RUG and was a continuation of my bachelor thesis.

**XAI-GP** · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17719) · [Code](https://github.com/matthjs/xai-gp)  
An empirical comparison of Gaussian processes and deep ensembles for uncertainty quantification, calibration, performance, and robustness to distribution shifts.

**Image-to-LaTeX for Mathematical Formulas** · [Code](https://github.com/matthjs/DL-Im2Latex)  
We reproduced a transformer-based OCR model that converts images of mathematical formulas into LaTeX, then fine-tuned it on handwritten equations and evaluated its performance on synthetic and out-of-distribution images.
